Sources, limits and credits

Where the listing data and photographs come from, how current they are, and what this reference cannot do.

What this is

An independent, open reference that helps customs, police, forestry and border officers screen timber against the CITES Appendices. It is not an official publication of the CITES Secretariat or of any government, and it carries no agency's endorsement.

Limits. End-grain features seen with a hand lens usually identify wood to genus or species group. They do not prove species. Listings, annotations, quotas and trade suspensions change. Before any enforcement action, confirm the listing on Species+ and have the wood identified by a qualified laboratory.
