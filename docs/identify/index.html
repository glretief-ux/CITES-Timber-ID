Photo identification trial

An experiment in recognising African timbers from a digital-microscope photo of the end grain. The model installed is a demonstration trained on four specimens. Its answers are not results.

Do not use for casework. This page shows how photo identification will work once a model has been trained and tested on verified specimens. For a real consignment use the 10x key and the species profiles. Photos are analysed in your browser and are not uploaded.
