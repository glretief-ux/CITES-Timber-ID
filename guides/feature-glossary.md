# Feature glossary — what you see at 10x on end grain

Each term links to a schematic of a CITES taxon that shows it well. Schematics are drawings, not photographs.

| Feature | What it looks like at 10x | Good example |
|---|---|---|
| **Softwood (no pores)** | Uniform mass of tiny cells; only growth rings and fine rays | [*Taxus*](../docs/schematics/taxus-spp.svg) |
| **Resin canals** | Scattered small light or dark dots, mostly in latewood (pines, spruces, larch, Douglas-fir only) | [*Pinus koraiensis*](../docs/schematics/pinus-koraiensis.svg) |
| **Gradual vs abrupt transition** | Earlywood fades slowly into latewood (soft pines) or changes sharply (hard pines, larch, yew) | *P. koraiensis* (gradual) vs *Taxus* (abrupt) |
| **Vesselless hardwood** | No pores, but rays wider than any softwood | [*Tetracentron*](../docs/schematics/tetracentron-sinense.svg) |
| **Diffuse porous** | Pores about the same size across the whole growth ring | [*Dalbergia*](../docs/schematics/dalbergia-spp.svg) |
| **Semi-ring porous** | Pores larger in earlywood, gradually smaller | [*Pterocarpus erinaceus*](../docs/schematics/pterocarpus-erinaceus.svg) |
| **Ring porous** | Distinct band of large earlywood pores, abrupt change to small latewood pores | [*Quercus mongolica*](../docs/schematics/quercus-mongolica.svg), [*Fraxinus*](../docs/schematics/fraxinus-mandshurica.svg), [*Cedrela*](../docs/schematics/cedrela-spp.svg) |
| **Solitary pores / radial multiples** | Pores alone, or in short radial chains of 2–5 | Multiples: [*Aquilaria*](../docs/schematics/aquilaria-gyrinops.svg) |
| **Tyloses** | Pores plugged with shiny, frothy cell walls | *Quercus* (white oaks) |
| **Deposits** | Coloured material in pores: yellow (ipê, *Afzelia*), white (*Swietenia*, iroko), dark red/black gum (rosewoods, mahoganies) | [*Afzelia*](../docs/schematics/afzelia-spp.svg), [ipê](../docs/schematics/ipe-handroanthus-tabebuia-roseodendron.svg) |
| **Vasicentric parenchyma** | Light halo completely surrounding the pore | [*Fraxinus*](../docs/schematics/fraxinus-mandshurica.svg) |
| **Aliform parenchyma** | Halo with lateral "wings" (lozenge/eye shape) | [*Dipteryx*](../docs/schematics/dipteryx-spp.svg) |
| **Confluent parenchyma** | Wings of neighbouring pores joined into short bands | [*Gonystylus*](../docs/schematics/gonystylus-spp.svg) |
| **Banded parenchyma** | Continuous, often wavy, light lines across the rays, independent of pores | [*Dalbergia*](../docs/schematics/dalbergia-spp.svg), [*Pterocarpus*](../docs/schematics/pterocarpus-africa.svg) |
| **Marginal parenchyma** | Single light line along the growth-ring boundary | [*Swietenia*](../docs/schematics/swietenia-spp.svg) |
| **Reticulate / diffuse-in-aggregates** | Very fine short tangential lines forming a net with the rays | [*Diospyros*](../docs/schematics/diospyros-madagascar.svg) |
| **Fine / broad rays** | Fine: barely visible lines; broad: clearly visible bands (oak) | Broad: *Quercus* |
| **Ripple marks** | On the **side** (tangential) face: fine horizontal lines, like a ruler, from storied rays/parenchyma | Rosewoods, *Pterocarpus*, ipê, cumaru, *Guaiacum* |
| **Included (interxylary) phloem** | Islands of soft dark tissue scattered in the wood | [*Aquilaria/Gyrinops*](../docs/schematics/aquilaria-gyrinops.svg) |

Further reading: Wheeler, Baas & Gasson (1989) *IAWA list of microscopic features for hardwood identification*; Ruffinatto & Crivellaro (2019) *Atlas of Macroscopic Wood Identification*; Wiedenhoeft (2011) *Identification of Central American Woods* (USDA FPL, free PDF).
