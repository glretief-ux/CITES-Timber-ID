# Field procedure — 10x macroscopic screening of suspect CITES timber

Purpose: decide quickly whether a consignment **may** contain CITES-listed wood, document it properly, and take samples for laboratory confirmation. Hand-lens identification is a **screening** step — it usually reaches genus or species group, not species.

## 1. Equipment
- 10x hand lens (loupe), ideally 10x–14x with LED light, or a smartphone with a clip-on 10x macro lens / XyloPhone-type adapter
- New single-edge razor blades or a very sharp utility knife (snap-off blades)
- Water spray bottle; small brush
- Scale ruler (mm) for photos
- Sample bags, labels, permanent marker, evidence seals
- Optional: UV torch (365 nm), 10% ammonia or NaOH solution (ipê lapachol test), small beaker of water (red-dye test for Pterocarpus/Paubrasilia)
- Gloves and dust mask (rosewoods, cocobolo, ipê dust are sensitising)

## 2. Choose the right spot
- Use the **end grain (transverse section)** — the cut end of a log, plank or block.
- Avoid sapwood, decayed, stained, painted or cracked areas. Choose **heartwood**.
- For finished products, look for an unobtrusive edge or underside; record any damage you cause.

## 3. Prepare a clean surface
1. Hold the blade at a low angle and make **one smooth slicing cut** across the end grain, removing a thin layer (≈ 1 × 1 cm area is enough).
2. Make 2–3 more overlapping cuts until the surface is shiny and smooth. Sawn or sanded surfaces hide pores — **always cut fresh**.
3. Lightly wet the surface: parenchyma and pores often become much clearer.

## 4. Observe in this order (matches the web key)
1. **Pores present?** No → softwood (or vesselless hardwood if rays are wide). Yes → hardwood.
2. **Porosity:** diffuse / semi-ring / ring porous.
3. **Pore size & frequency;** solitary or in radial multiples.
4. **Pore contents:** yellow, white, dark gum, tyloses.
5. **Axial parenchyma pattern:** halo, wings/lozenge, joined (confluent), fine bands, marginal lines, net.
6. **Rays:** fine / medium / broad.
7. Tangential (side) face: **ripple marks**?
8. Softwoods: **resin canals**? earlywood–latewood transition abrupt or gradual?
9. Physical: colour of heartwood/sapwood, weight in hand (does a chip sink?), smell on a fresh cut, oily feel.

Then use `docs/index.html` (the web key) or `species/README.md` to compare with listed taxa **and their look-alikes**.

## 5. Quick chemical/physical tests
| Test | How | Positive result suggests |
|---|---|---|
| Lapachol | 1 drop 10% ammonia/NaOH on yellow pore deposits | Turns deep red → *Handroanthus* (ipê) |
| Water dye | Shavings in clear water, 5 min | Red/orange colour → *Pterocarpus santalinus*/*tinctorius*, *Paubrasilia*, other red dye woods |
| UV fluorescence | 365 nm torch on fresh end grain / water extract | Useful to separate some look-alikes; compare with known reference only |
| Float | Small heartwood chip in water | Sinks → density > 1,000 kg/m³ (e.g. *P. santalinus*, *Dalbergia melanoxylon*, *Guaiacum*, ipê, cumaru) |
| Burn | Smouldering sliver (outdoors) | Strong sweet incense → agarwood (*Aquilaria/Gyrinops*) |

## 6. Document
- Photograph: whole consignment, markings, each sampled item, the cut end grain at 10x **with scale**, dry and wet.
- Record: location, date/time, officer, item IDs, declared species/HS code, origin, documents (permit numbers), GPS.
- Fill in a row per sample in your case file using the same feature terms (see [feature glossary](feature-glossary.md)).

## 7. Sample for the laboratory
- **Wood anatomy:** block ≈ 2 × 2 × 5 cm of heartwood, or a 5 cm long sliver.
- **DNA:** heartwood with as little contamination as possible; drill shavings into clean bag, avoid treated/old surfaces; change blade/gloves between samples.
- **Mass spectrometry (DART-TOF) / NIR:** small heartwood splinters (matchstick size) are enough.
- Seal, label, and keep chain-of-custody records. Keep a reference part of each sample.

## 8. Decide
- Features **consistent with a CITES taxon** and documents missing/inconsistent → detain and send for confirmation.
- Features clearly **inconsistent** with declared species → mis-declaration; investigate.
- Remember **annotations**: some listings cover only logs/sawn wood/veneer (e.g. #5, #6, #17); others cover finished products (no annotation, #2, #4, #15 with 10 kg rule).
- Remember **population limits**: e.g. *Khaya*, *Afzelia*, *Pterocarpus* (Africa only); *Cedrela*, *Swietenia macrophylla* (Neotropics only); *Diospyros* (Madagascar only); App. III (listing country only).
